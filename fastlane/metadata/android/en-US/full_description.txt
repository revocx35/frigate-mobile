Frigate Mobile is an unofficial, native Android client for the Frigate network video recorder. It talks straight to your own Frigate server; there is no cloud service in between and no tracking.

Features:
* Cameras: every camera with a frame that refreshes every 2 seconds while you look, online status and recording/detection badges.
* Live view: the camera's own stream via Frigate's go2rtc (full resolution and frame rate, optional sound, main/sub stream), or Frigate's MJPEG stream as a fallback. Full screen in landscape.
* PTZ: pan, tilt, zoom and presets for ONVIF cameras.
* Review: alerts and detections, newest first, filtered to unreviewed items and by camera. Mark all as reviewed.
* Review item: plays the recording around the event, lists the tracked objects with snapshots, and marks it reviewed.
* Recordings: browse a camera's recordings by day and hour and play them (seekable HLS).
* Camera switches for admins: object detection, recording, snapshots and audio detection (Frigate 0.17+).
* System: Frigate version and update notice, detector speed, CPU/GPU load, storage and camera FPS.
* Optional alert notifications with thumbnails (checked every 15 minutes).

Security:
* HTTPS only. Frigate's self-signed certificate (port 8971) is pinned after you compare its SHA-256 fingerprint.
* The session token and (only if you choose "stay signed in") your password are encrypted with an Android Keystore key. App backups are off.
* Session cookie is only ever sent to your Frigate server.
