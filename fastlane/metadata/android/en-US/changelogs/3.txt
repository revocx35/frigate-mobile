New: PTZ control for ONVIF cameras (hold the arrows to pan/tilt, zoom, presets), also in full screen.
Live view now plays the camera's own stream through Frigate's go2rtc: full resolution and frame rate, optional sound, and a choice between main and sub stream. Cameras without a go2rtc stream fall back to MJPEG.
The camera grid now refreshes every 2 seconds and no longer gets stuck on an old frame.
